# Ideas for later

Not built yet. Each would go behind its own flag in `T.FEATURES`, like the rest.
Standing rules: nothing fake presented as real data, events stay rare and quiet, copy, palette and fonts stay as they are.

## Terminal

- **Egg counter.** `eggs` shows how many easter eggs the visitor has found (`4/14 found`), stored in their browser. Finding all of them unlocks a reward: a fourth phosphor, or a hidden Deck E.
- **The ship as a text adventure.** `look`, `go deck c`, `open airlock`, built on `map`. The airlock tilts the page while Camille clings on.
- **`hail`.** Composing a message to Yam plays as a transmission with a send animation, then opens the visitor's mail client.

## Screens and effects

- **Publications as transmissions.** The ORCID publication list arrives as received messages with their real dates.
- **Light pen.** Click and drag on empty tube to leave a phosphor trail that fades.
- **Burn-in ghosts.** A faint afterimage of what a screen showed for a long time.
- **A fourth phosphor.** Possibly as the egg counter's reward.
- **Sound for the newer things.** Sound stays off unless the visitor turns it on.

## Cats

- **Guenhwyvar.** The locomotion patch written earlier was never integrated. Its own notes say the walk is an appearance fix, not a corrected gait.
- **Drizzt, next art.** A frame or two of him moving between his stance alone and his stance beside the panther, and a sheathe from that stance, so he can put the scimitars away when he leaves with her. No walk exists; he arrives and leaves in the dark.
- **A drawn bug.** The four-pose crawl and catch spark, redrawn at 8x6 logical pixels to match the bug the page draws now.

## Housekeeping

- **Offline.** A service worker so the page loads without a link.
