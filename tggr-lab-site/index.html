TGGR Lab

Translational Genetics and Genomics Research Laboratory
Genetics Institute, Tel Aviv Sourasky Medical Center

We look for the genes behind rare inherited disease in families referred to the Genetics Institute, and work out what those genes do. The software and hardware we build along the way is below.

Tools

  • PellaeonTalk to UCSF ChimeraX in plain English.
  • GPCompaReResidue contacts in active and inactive states of 283 human Class A GPCRs.
  • CO₂ monitorA CO₂ and temperature monitor for cell culture incubators, built on an ESP32.
  • Bench MateBench calculators and protocols for molecular biology, on your phone.
