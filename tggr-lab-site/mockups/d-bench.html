Protocol 01 · how a project runs

Gene discovery, one family

  1. Family referred to the Genetics Institute. Pedigree drawn at intake: consanguineous, 2 affected
  2. Trio exome, in house timer 4 wk
  3. Filter to variants that segregate with disease. Candidates: 1
  4. Functional assay tailored to the gene timer 6–18 mo
  5. Diagnosis to the family. Counselling. Write it up.
