Rare disease, one family at a time. Open tools, one repo at a time.

We look for the genes behind rare disease in the families who come to the Genetics Institute, then work out what those genes do. The code we write on the way is public.

github.com/tggr-labThe lab at Ichilov

Research

The starting point is always a family: people referred with a rare, often inherited condition that existing tests could not explain. From there the work runs the same way every time.

  • 01Families referred to the Genetics Institute, many consanguineous, which makes recessive disease easier to map.
  • 02Exome and genome sequencing in house, and the analysis to find variants that segregate with disease.
  • 03Functional work tailored to the gene: cell models, animal models, protein structure.
  • 04Back to clinic: a diagnosis, counselling, a new gene in the literature, sometimes a target.
