TggR

Translational Genetics & Genomics Research Laboratory

Genetics Institute, Tel Aviv Sourasky Medical Center, and the Faculty of Medicine, Tel Aviv University. Head: Prof. Hagit Baris Feldman. The lab studies rare, mostly monogenic disease in families referred to the Genetics Institute. It finds the causative gene by exome and genome sequencing, then characterises what the gene does. This page lists the software and hardware the lab has released.

A database of receptor contacts, with reports

What changes when a receptor switches on. Residue contacts in the inactive and active states of 283 human Class A G protein-coupled receptors, compared one by one.

An instrument, built on an ESP32

A CO₂ and temperature monitor for a cell culture incubator, made from three parts and six wires, with a build guide and ten days of real data. Calibrate before relying on it.

How a project runsfour steps, family to diagnosis

  1. FamiliesReferred to the Genetics Institute with a rare, often inherited condition. Many are consanguineous, which makes recessive disease easier to map.
  2. SequencingExome and genome sequencing in house, and the analysis that finds the variants segregating with disease.
  3. FunctionCell and animal models, protein structure, and assays made for the gene in question.
  4. ClinicA diagnosis for the family, counselling, a new gene in the literature, and where the mechanism allows it, a target.
