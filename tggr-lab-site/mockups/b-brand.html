TGGR Lab
Translational Genetics and Genomics Research

The lab studies rare, mostly monogenic disease in families referred to the Genetics Institute. It finds the causative gene by exome and genome sequencing, then characterises what the gene does. This page lists the software and hardware the lab has released. Head: Prof. Hagit Baris Feldman.
